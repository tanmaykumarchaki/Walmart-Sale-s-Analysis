# 📊 Walmart Sales Analysis (Power BI)

This Power BI project analyzes Walmart's sales data from **2011 to 2014**, focusing on identifying trends, top-performing products, and geographical performance. The goal is to derive actionable business insights and visualize the key performance indicators in an interactive format.

## 🔍 Project Overview

- **Objective**: Analyze Walmart’s historical sales and profit data to understand which products and regions contribute most to revenue.
- **Tool Used**: Microsoft Power BI
- **Time Period**: 2011 - 2014
- **Data Source**: Walmart Sales Dataset (details redacted)

---

## 📈 Key Dashboards

### 1. **Geographical Performance**
- **Visuals**: Bar charts
- **Insights**: 
  - Top cities by **max sales** and **max profit** include:
    - Los Angeles, CA
    - Seattle, WA
    - Sacramento, CA
  - Seattle had the highest sales (~14K), followed by LA (~6.7K).

### 2. **Top Products Analysis**
- **Visuals**: Donut charts, column charts
- **Insights**:
  - Top 5 products by quantity sold:
    - Fellowes PB500 Electric Punch
    - Canon PC940 Copier
    - Canon Image Class D660 Copier
  - Products like Canon imageCLASS and Fellowes PB500 had high average sales and profit.

### 3. **Yearly Trends**
- **Visuals**: Line/column combo chart
- **Insights**:
  - Both **Sales** and **Profit** steadily increased from 2011 to 2014.
  - 2014 saw the peak in overall performance.

### 4. **Category and Quantity Analysis**
- **Visuals**: Clustered column charts
- **Insights**:
  - **Copiers** and **Binders** showed high profits with moderate quantity.
  - Profitability varies significantly across product categories and quantities.

---

## 📊 Metrics Tracked

- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Quantity Sold
- Sales and Profit by:
  - Year
  - Geography (City, State)
  - Product Name
  - Product Category

---

## 💡 Insights Summary

- Seattle and Los Angeles dominate in both sales and profit.
- Specific electronic products and binding machines deliver the highest returns.
- Profitability doesn't always scale with quantity—emphasizing the importance of margin-rich products.
- Strategic stocking and marketing in profitable regions and for high-margin items can boost revenue.
